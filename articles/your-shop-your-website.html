Your Shop + Your Website: Build a Digital Storefront That Works for You

A social media page can help people discover your business. A website gives those visitors a place you control: a clear catalogue, service details, contact options and, when configured, an online checkout.

A storefront that stays organised

Present products, service packages, prices, frequently asked questions and business details in a consistent format. Visitors can browse without searching through old posts.

A clearer path from interest to purchase

A well-planned listing can guide a visitor from product details to an enquiry or checkout. Payment options should be secure, transparent and tested before launch.

Your website works alongside your existing channels

Keep using social media and marketplaces for discovery. Link them to your own website so customers have a central place to learn about your offer.
