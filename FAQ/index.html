Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about services, orders and using the KasuhaNET platform. Where a detail depends on project scope, the final terms will be confirmed before work begins.

General Information

What is KasuhaNET.STORE?

KasuhaNET.STORE is a digital services and technical resource platform operated by KasuhaNET Resources. It brings together website services, digital support, technical guides and content related to online business.

Does KasuhaNET work with individuals and businesses?

Yes. Projects can be discussed for individuals, entrepreneurs, organisations and businesses. The proposed solution depends on the project goal, required functions, content and budget.

Website Development

How much does a website cost?

The reference prices are RM299 for Landing Page and RM900 for Premium Standard. When a promotion is active, the current discounted prices appear on the sales page and checkout. Package scope is listed on the sales page; domain and hosting are charged separately.

How long does website development take?

Timing depends on scope, supplied materials and customer feedback. A simple project may take around 3 to 7 working days when all required content and details are ready. More complex projects can take longer.

Can I request custom features or extra pages?

Yes. Additional features such as forms, catalogues, payment integrations or specific modules must be reviewed first. Any scope or cost changes will be explained before work proceeds.

Custom Web Workshop

How is the Custom Web Workshop different from a standard website package?

The Custom Web Workshop focuses on adding or modifying components on an existing website, subject to technical suitability and the agreed scope.

Does my existing website need to be rebuilt?

Not necessarily. The existing structure and code are reviewed first. If suitable, improvements can be made without rebuilding the entire website.

What should I prepare before submitting a request?

Prepare your website URL, a list of requested changes, any useful reference examples, and the access or files needed for the work. Do not send passwords through public forms or ordinary messages.

Domain, Hosting & Maintenance

Are domain and hosting included in every package?

No. Domain registration and hosting are not included in either package. Customers may use an existing domain or request a separate quotation for domain registration, hosting and configuration.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes, subject to DNS access and configuration compatibility. Make sure you control the domain account before the connection process begins.

Payments & Orders

Which payment methods are available?

Checkout options currently displayed include Stripe and ToyyibPay, subject to availability for the relevant product or page. The options shown at checkout are the ones available for that order.

When will work begin after payment?

Orders are reviewed against payment confirmation and project details. Work begins after the essential information, materials and agreed scope have been received.

Support & After-Sales

How can I get help?

Use Live Chat Support or the Support Centre page to submit an enquiry. Include your order reference when relevant, and never share passwords, PINs or one-time passcodes.

Is after-sales support available?

Basic post-handover support may be provided according to the scope and period stated in the project agreement. New features or major changes may be treated as additional work.

Affiliate & Third-Party Links

Are all products and links on the website operated by KasuhaNET?

No. Some pages contain partner, referral or affiliate links to external providers. The external provider's terms, payment processes and privacy policy apply when you use its service.

Does KasuhaNET earn commissions from affiliate links?

Some links may provide a commission or reward to KasuhaNET at no additional cost to the user. Affiliate disclosures are provided on relevant pages.

Package Scope & Additional Services

Does the RM299 starting offer include a domain, hosting or VPS?

No. The starting offer does not automatically include a domain, web hosting or VPS. These are separate services and can be discussed as optional requirements. The written quotation will state exactly what is included before you confirm the order.

Is payment gateway integration included in the starting offer?

Not by default. Payment gateway integration is an additional requirement that must be reviewed for provider compatibility, account readiness and project scope. Any extra cost will be stated before the work is approved.

How are extra changes or features handled after the project scope is agreed?

Requests outside the agreed scope are reviewed separately. We will explain the impact on cost and delivery time and obtain your approval before proceeding with additional work.

Google Search & Website Visibility

Can KasuhaNET guarantee a first-page Google ranking?

No. Search rankings depend on many factors outside any developer's control, including competition, content quality, site history and Google's systems. We can discuss suitable SEO improvements, but no ranking position should be promised.

Will my new website appear on Google immediately?

Not necessarily. Google must crawl and index a website, and the timing varies. Sitemap submission and Search Console tools can help discovery, but they do not guarantee immediate indexing or a particular ranking.

Project Preparation & Security

What information should I provide to request a quotation?

Share your current website URL if available, the main goal, requested pages or functions, reference examples, preferred timeline and approximate budget. Clear requirements help us assess the work more accurately.

Do I need to share my passwords or banking details?

No. Never send passwords, banking PINs, card security codes or one-time passwords through chat or public forms. If access is required, use a limited or temporary account where possible and revoke it after the work is complete.

Can I request improvements to my existing live website?

Yes, subject to a technical review. Before changes are made, the existing setup, access and backup arrangements should be checked. Where possible, changes should be tested in a staging environment before being applied to the live website.

Website Package Details

What is included in the Landing Page package?

It includes one responsive page with hero, about, services or products and contact sections, a WhatsApp button, one basic enquiry form, and basic SEO settings such as the page title and meta description. Domain and hosting are not included.

What is included in Premium Standard?

Premium includes a website with up to five main pages, such as Home, About, Services, Portfolio or Gallery, and Contact. It also includes responsive design, a gallery, social links, an enquiry form, a five-page content structure; a CMS or admin panel is an optional add-on if required, and basic on-page SEO and sitemap setup. Custom features must be confirmed in the quotation.

Are domain registration and hosting included in either package?

No. Domain registration and hosting are separate from both Landing Page and Premium Standard. You may use a domain you already own or request a separate quotation for domain registration, hosting and configuration.

Are payment gateway integration or a full online store included in Premium?

Not by default. Payment gateway integration, e-commerce catalogues, shopping carts and full order management are additional features that must be reviewed and quoted separately before development.

How does the current promotion price work?

The reference prices are RM299 for Landing Page and RM900 for Premium Standard. While a promotion is active, the applicable discounted prices are displayed on the sales page and checkout. Prices return to the reference rates after the promotion ends. Domain, hosting and additional features are excluded.
