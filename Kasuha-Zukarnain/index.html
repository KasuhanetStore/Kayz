Founder & Developer

Kasuha Zukarnain

Founder of Kasuhanet Resources and developer behind KasuhaNET.STORE.

Kasuha Zukarnain is the founder and developer behind KasuhaNET.STORE, a Malaysia-based digital services platform. His work focuses on website development, custom web improvements, digital tools, Linux and terminal environments, automation, and software integration.

He also established the Kasuha Oculus Organisation identity and develops the Kasuha Oculus framework as part of a wider digital systems and business initiative.
