Kasuha Zukarnain

Real name: Shafiq Zukarnain.

Principal Founder of Kasuha Okulus and founder behind the KasuhaNET digital platform.

Kasuha Zukarnain is the founder and developer behind KasuhaNET.STORE, a Malaysia-based digital services platform. His work focuses on website development, custom web improvements, digital tools, Linux and terminal environments, automation, and software integration.

He is the principal founder of Kasuha Okulus, also known as KZ Organisation. He leads the identity and direction behind KasuhaNET.STORE, the online business platform operated by Kasuhanet Resources.
