End-to-End Workflow & Service Terms

End-to-End Workflow

  1. Package purchase: Choose the Landing Page or Premium option and complete payment using the provided link.
  2. Briefing session: Contact us through the agreed communication channel. Details such as your logo, text, product images and social links will be required.
  3. Development & design: Code structure, visual design and integrations are built according to the package.
  4. Review & testing: A preview link is provided for review; mobile display and page performance are tested.
  5. Launch & handover: The website is published on the agreed domain. Administrator access and documentation are handed over if included in the scope.
