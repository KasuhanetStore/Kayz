Privacy Policy

KasuhaNET Resources · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what information may be collected when you browse KasuhaNET.STORE, contact our support team or place an order, and how that information is used.

1. Who operates this website

KasuhaNET.STORE is operated by KasuhaNET Resources (business reference 202503248308), based in Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia. For privacy questions or requests, contact kasuhaokulusorganisation@kasuhanet.store.

2. Information we may collect

  • Contact and support details you submit, such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.
  • Order details needed to process a purchase, such as your name, email address, phone number, selected package, order reference and payment status.
  • Basic technical information that may be processed by the website host or security services, such as IP address, browser information, request logs and timestamps.

3. How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, provide support, process and reconcile orders, maintain website security, prevent misuse, meet legal obligations and improve the services. We do not intentionally request full payment-card numbers or security codes through our website forms.

4. Payment and service providers

When you make a payment, Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy terms. KasuhaNET may receive transaction references and payment status needed to manage your order. Cloudflare hosting, Cloudflare D1 database infrastructure, security and support services may also process information on our behalf to provide the service.

5. Cookies, advertising and similar technologies

The website or its service providers may use essential cookies or similar technologies for security, basic functionality and preferences. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to the visitor's settings, applicable consent requirements and Google's policies.

Visitors can manage cookies through their browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website functions. For information about Google's advertising practices, review Google's published privacy and advertising documentation.

6. Affiliate and external links

Some pages contain links to third-party providers, referral programmes or affiliate services. When you follow those links, the third party's website and privacy policy apply. We may receive a referral fee or reward where the programme provides one. We do not control how external providers collect or use your information.

7. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate the website, payment providers, professional advisers or authorities where required by law. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including order records, security and legal requirements.

8. Security and your choices

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Children and changes

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
